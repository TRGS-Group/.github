**TRGS is a multidisciplinary studio working across marketing, AI, and technology.**
We bring research, design, engineering, and growth into the same conversation—then
carry the work from the first question to a working system.

Our work spans digital experiences, AI applications, business systems, and the
strategy that connects them to people's ambitions.

### Four disciplines. One adaptive identity.

| | What it brings to the work |
| :--- | :--- |
| **T · Technology** | Translate ideas into products, tools, and systems people can use. |
| **R · Research** | Examine assumptions, understand the problem, and find a sound direction. |
| **G · Growth** | Connect what we build to how a business reaches people and develops. |
| **S · The dynamic variable** | Solutions, Science, Studio, Strategies, Systems, Subjective. The form follows the problem. |

### Subjective Initiative

A stable core. An adaptable approach. We align the disciplines around what the
problem requires, taking ownership of both the thinking and the execution.

The purpose is human: give the people we work with the capability and freedom to
act on their own intentions. Better systems should help you decide, create, and
move forward on your terms.
